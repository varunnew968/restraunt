Our Royal Heritage

PRESERVING THE NIZAMI CULINARY ARTISTRY

Established in Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad, Saffron & Spice was born out of a passion to revive forgotten royal recipes from the kitchens of Nizam khansamas. Every Biryani is slow-cooked over wood charcoal in traditional handis sealed with dough.

We source our Saffron directly from Pampore (Kashmir), our Rose Water from Kannauj, and mix our Garam Masalas in-house daily to guarantee authentic rich aromas that transport you to royal banquets.
